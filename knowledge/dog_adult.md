Title: Adult dog care basics
Species: dog
Stage: adult
Keywords: dog, adult, walk, exercise, feeding, brushing, enrichment, teeth

- Feed 2 meals a day (10 min each), about 12 hours apart.
- Walk 2 times a day, 20-30 minutes each; high-energy breeds (labrador, retriever, border collie, husky, shepherd) need 60+ minutes total of exercise.
- Play or training session daily (15 min) for mental enrichment.
- Brush coat 2-3 times a week (10 min); long-haired breeds (poodle, golden retriever, collie) need daily brushing.
- Brush teeth or give a dental chew daily (5 min).
- Weekly: nail check and ear check (10 min).
