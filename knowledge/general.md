Title: General pet care checklist
Species: any
Stage: all
Keywords: pet, care, water, feeding, vet, checkup, routine, health

- Keep feeding and medication at the same times every day; consistency reduces stress.
- Fresh water available at all times; refresh daily (2 min).
- Quick daily health check: appetite, energy, and bathroom habits (5 min).
- Schedule an annual vet checkup and keep vaccines up to date.
- Spend dedicated one-on-one time with your pet every day (10-15 min).
