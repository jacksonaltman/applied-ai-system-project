Title: Hamster, guinea pig and small mammal care
Species: small mammal
Stage: all
Keywords: hamster, guinea pig, gerbil, rat, mouse, small mammal, cage, wheel, hay

- Fresh food and water daily (5 min); guinea pigs also need daily vitamin C vegetables and unlimited hay.
- Spot-clean the cage daily (5 min); full cage clean weekly (20 min).
- Supervised out-of-cage time or play 15-30 minutes daily; hamsters are nocturnal, so keep interaction in the evening.
- Handle gently for 5-10 minutes daily to build trust.
- Check teeth, nails, and weight weekly (5 min).
