Title: Senior dog care basics
Species: dog
Stage: senior
Keywords: senior, old, elderly, dog, arthritis, joints, medication, vet, gentle

- Feed 2 smaller meals a day (10 min each); keep the schedule consistent.
- Replace one long walk with 2-3 gentle short walks of 10-15 minutes; avoid hard running and jumping.
- Give any joint supplements or medication at the same times every day (5 min).
- Check mobility, lumps, and weight weekly (10 min).
- Gentle brushing daily (10 min); senior coats and skin need extra care.
- Schedule vet checkups every 6 months.
