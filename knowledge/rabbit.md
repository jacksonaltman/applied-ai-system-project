Title: Rabbit care basics
Species: rabbit
Stage: all
Keywords: rabbit, bunny, hay, greens, hutch, chew, exercise

- Unlimited hay all day; refresh hay and water daily (5 min).
- Fresh leafy greens once or twice a day (5 min) and a small portion of pellets.
- At least 3-4 hours of out-of-cage exercise daily, split into two sessions (30 min minimum each).
- Spot-clean the litter area daily (5 min) and fully clean the enclosure weekly (20 min).
- Brush 1-2 times a week, daily during shedding (10 min).
- Offer chew toys; check teeth and nails regularly.
