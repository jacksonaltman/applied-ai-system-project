Title: Reptile care basics
Species: reptile
Stage: all
Keywords: reptile, lizard, snake, gecko, bearded dragon, turtle, tortoise, heat lamp, uvb, habitat

- Check temperature, humidity, and lighting every morning (5 min); keep a warm basking spot and a cooler side.
- Feed on the schedule for the species: juveniles daily, many adults every 2-3 days (10 min).
- Fresh water daily (5 min); spot-clean waste daily (5 min).
- Full habitat clean monthly (45 min).
- Gentle handling 2-3 times a week, 10 minutes, and never right after feeding.
- Lights on a consistent 10-12 hour daily cycle.
