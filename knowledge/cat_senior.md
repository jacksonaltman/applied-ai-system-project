Title: Senior cat care basics
Species: cat
Stage: senior
Keywords: senior, old, elderly, cat, kidney, weight, medication, vet, gentle

- Feed 2-3 small meals a day (10 min each) and watch appetite and water intake.
- Scoop the litter box daily (5 min); make sure the box has low sides for easy access.
- Gentle play sessions of 5-10 minutes, once or twice a day.
- Give medication at consistent times (5 min).
- Brush gently 3-4 times a week (10 min); older cats groom less.
- Schedule vet checkups every 6 months, including bloodwork.
