Title: Fish care basics
Species: fish
Stage: all
Keywords: fish, aquarium, tank, goldfish, betta, water, filter, feeding

- Feed once or twice a day in small amounts (5 min); do not overfeed.
- Quick daily check of temperature, filter, and fish behavior (5 min).
- Weekly partial water change of 20-30 percent (20 min) and test water parameters.
- Clean the glass and check the filter media monthly (20 min).
