Title: Puppy care basics
Species: dog
Stage: young
Keywords: puppy, dog, training, socialization, potty, teething, vaccines

- Feed 3 meals a day (about 15 min each), spaced roughly 5-6 hours apart.
- Potty break after every meal, nap, and play session; at minimum every 2 hours (10 min).
- Several short walks or play sessions of 10-15 minutes each; avoid long runs while joints develop.
- Daily training in 5-10 minute sessions (sit, recall, leash manners).
- Socialization and gentle handling practice daily (10 min).
- Puppies need 14-18 hours of sleep: schedule quiet crate or nap time.
