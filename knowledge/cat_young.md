Title: Kitten care basics
Species: cat
Stage: young
Keywords: kitten, cat, play, litter, vaccines, socialization, growth

- Feed 3-4 small meals a day (10 min each).
- Interactive play 3-4 times a day, 10 min each (wand toys, chasing).
- Scoop the litter box at least twice a day (5 min).
- Gentle handling and socialization daily (10 min).
- Provide scratching posts and climbing spots; supervise exploration.
- Schedule vaccines and deworming with the vet as recommended.
