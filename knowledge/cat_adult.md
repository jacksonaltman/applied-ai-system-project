Title: Adult cat care basics
Species: cat
Stage: adult
Keywords: cat, adult, litter, play, feeding, brushing, indoor, enrichment

- Feed 2 meals a day (10 min each); fresh water daily.
- Scoop the litter box daily (5 min) and fully change litter weekly (15 min).
- Interactive play 1-2 times a day, 10-15 minutes each, to prevent boredom and weight gain.
- Brush 1-2 times a week (10 min); long-haired breeds (persian, maine coon, ragdoll) need daily brushing.
- Rotate toys and offer a window perch for enrichment.
- Weekly: nail trim check (5 min).
