Title: Bird care basics
Species: bird
Stage: all
Keywords: bird, parrot, parakeet, budgie, cockatiel, canary, cage, social, foraging

- Fresh food and water daily, morning and evening (5-10 min each).
- Out-of-cage social time and flying or climbing: 1-3 hours daily, in sessions of 30 min or more.
- Spot-clean cage bottom daily (5 min); full cage clean weekly (20 min).
- Foraging toys and training or talking sessions daily (10-15 min).
- Provide 10-12 hours of dark, quiet sleep at night; cover the cage if needed.
